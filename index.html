The Divine Legend

Why Lord Jagannath
Loves Poda Pitha

During the annual Rath Yatra, Lord Jagannath travels from the Jagannath Temple to Gundicha Temple. On his return journey — Bahuda Yatra — he pauses at the Mausi Maa Temple, where Poda Pitha is offered as the sacred bhog. It is believed that in a past life as Shri Ram, the Lord had promised to return and savour this beloved cake. That divine promise lives on in every bite.

"If the Lord adores it, then the best kind of Pitha ought to be made in Puri."

Poda Pitha preparations begin on Hera Panchami and continue through the eve of Bahuda Yatra. Made from fermented rice, black gram, grated coconut and jaggery — then slow-baked overnight — it develops a slightly charred crust while remaining soft and white inside. "Poda" means burnt in Odia, and that beautiful char is exactly what makes it divine.

The Art of the Burnt Cake

How Poda Pitha
is Made

🌾
Step 01
Soak & Ferment
Rice and black gram (urad dal) are soaked overnight and ground into a coarse, fermented batter — the soul of the pitha.
🥥
Step 02
Mix the Goodness
Jaggery, grated coconut, ghee, cardamom, black pepper, and cloves are folded in. Dry fruits added for festive batches.
🍃
Step 03
Wrap in Banana Leaf
The batter is poured into a banana-leaf-lined earthen vessel. The leaf imparts a subtle fragrance and natural sweetness.
🔥
Step 04
Slow-Bake Overnight
Over a low charcoal flame for 11–12 hours. The outer crust chars beautifully while the inside remains soft and white.
Poda Pitha & Odisha's Festivals

A Sacred Presence
in Every Celebration

In the land of thirteen festivals in twelve months, Poda Pitha is woven into the fabric of the most sacred celebrations.

🛕
July — Annual Pilgrimage
Rath Yatra & Bahuda Yatra
Lord Jagannath halts at Mausi Maa Temple on his return journey and is offered Poda Pitha — a tradition rooted in a promise made lifetimes ago. Preparations begin at Hera Panchami and continue until the eve of Bahuda Yatra.
🌧️
June — Monsoon Arrival
Raja Parba
The three-day festival celebrating womanhood and the fertility of Mother Earth. Poda Pitha is the undisputed heart of Raja — families gather to bake it on earthen chulhas, passing down recipes through generations of mothers and grandmothers.
🌾
January — Harvest Season
Makar Sankranti
The rice harvest festival features Poda Pitha as a special delicacy, celebrating the bounty of the land. Freshly harvested rice goes into the batter, connecting the cake to Odisha's deeply agrarian roots.
🙏
Daily Ritual
Jagannath Temple Mahaprasad
Poda Pitha is offered daily to Lord Jagannath at the Shri Mandir. The temple is one of the Char Dham pilgrimage sites, and its Mahaprasad — including Poda Pitha — is considered blessed food that carries the Lord's grace.
